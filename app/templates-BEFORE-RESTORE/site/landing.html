Built on a real, verifiable pipeline

Every number on this site comes from an actual 10-stage processing pipeline run against real satellite data — not a simulation or a mockup.

🛰️

Real satellite imagery

Raw Sentinel-2 Level-2A imagery, cloud-masked and processed to compute NDVI vegetation health for both time periods.

📐

Precise change detection

NDVI difference thresholding isolates genuine vegetation loss from normal seasonal variation, then maps it to real hectare areas.

🌲

Forest boundary aware

Every loss area is checked against real land-cover data to determine if it's inside protected forest or outside it.

🔗

Tamper-evident records

Forest-loss evidence is hashed and chained block-by-block. Any later change to the evidence is detectable.

🏛️

Government dashboard

Interactive map, full evidence packages, and blockchain verification for the Forest Department.

🤝

NGO awareness view

A separate, simpler view of outside-forest loss for community action and restoration planning.
